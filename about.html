MORE ABOUT ME

Introduction

Hi, I'm Khanh, otherwise known as Yue. I'm a 19-year-old student at RMIT University, majoring in Digital Media. I'm also a self-taught photographer and artist, currently open for commissions.

I have a deep passion for art, especially drawing and illustration. As someone who is naturally quite shy, I feel that art gives me a way to express my personality and show my own colors without having to put everything into words.

I love exploring different forms of art and find myself easily drawn to new creative mediums and styles. I'm always excited to experiment, learn new things, and step outside of what I'm already familiar with.

Experience

I have been involved in art from a very young age. As a child, I often took part in drawing and design competitions and achieved several accomplishments that encouraged me to continue pursuing art.

As I grew older, I began dedicating much of my time to studying art, exploring different styles, and learning about its history. I have also gained five years of experience working as a freelance artist.

The programs I use most often for drawing and editing are ibisPaint X, Adobe Photoshop, and Adobe Illustrator.
